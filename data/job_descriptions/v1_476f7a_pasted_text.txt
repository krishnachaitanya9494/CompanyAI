Job Description:
•	Scheduling and Organizing Shortlisted Candidates Interviews
•	Coordinate with hiring managers, interview panels, and suppliers to schedule shortlisted candidate interviews
•	Send interview invitations to panels and provide necessary information (location, time, mode, etc.)
•	Set up virtual or in-person interview requests
•	Manage Interview Drives (Zoom or Walk-ins)
•	Monitor interview schedules and follow up on feedback from interviewers.
•	Update candidate status in the procurement tool (Shortlist or Rejects) and share the feedback with vendors
•	Assist with the next steps, whether it's scheduling additional interviews or communicating decisions
•	Publish candidate pool statuses to leadership
•	Support on Adhoc operational and reporting tasks
Minimum Requirements:
•	Experience: 1 to 3 Yrs
•	Education: Minimum of 3 Yrs Degree
•	Location: C2, Manyata
•	Strong organizational and time management skills.
•	Excellent communication skills (both verbal and written).
•	Microsoft Office - Word/Excel/Outlook
•	Proficiency in managing or scheduling candidates via VMS tool
•	Ability to multitask and handle multiple interviews simultaneously.
•	High attention to detail and a proactive approach.
•	Ability to work with both vendors and internal teams
