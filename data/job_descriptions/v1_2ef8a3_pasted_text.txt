Job Description – Contract Resource, Global Tax
Role summary
We need a motivated graduate with an interest in tax to support the Global Tax team. The role covers India Tax compliance for the India entity and Sales and Use Tax (SUT) matters for Target's US entities. You'll get hands-on exposure to both Indian and US indirect tax processes and work closely with internal teams and external advisors.
Key responsibilities
India Tax
•	Support routine compliance for TDS, GST and income tax, including data collection, workings and preparing returns and challans.
•	Prepare reconciliations, such as GST and TDS against the books and Form 26AS/AIS.
•	Gather documents and data for tax audits, assessments and notice responses.
•	Keep compliance trackers, calendars and supporting files up to date.
•	Coordinate with Finance, Procurement and vendors to collect information and resolve queries.
US Sales and Use Tax
•	Help prepare SUT returns and supporting workings for the US entities.
•	Reconcile SUT data with the general ledger and source reports.
•	Maintain exemption certificates and records.
•	Support SUT notices, data requests and audit documentation.
General
•	Extract and analyse data from SAP and other systems.
•	Document processes and help make routine tasks more efficient.
•	Work with external advisors on data and documentation requests.
